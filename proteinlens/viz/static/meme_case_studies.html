  • Geometry vs MEME Case Studies

Families of SAE nodes whose top MEME/PWM motif is significant (BH-corrected q < 0.05) and which also have significant geometry PR-AUC (q < 0.05). Because MEME consensuses are rarely identical across features, nodes are grouped when their best motif differs by at most a few residues (edit distance). Within each family, nodes share the same sequence motif but may encode different geometric properties — evidence that identical primary-sequence signatures can resolve into distinct structural roles.
