Most geometry-primary features fire on proteins from a known InterPro family (high protein-level F1), but their residue-level activation patterns cannot be explained by the domain boundaries (low residue F1). When multiple geometry-primary features share the same InterPro annotation, each captures a distinct geometric sub-structure within the domain — decomposing a single annotation into fine-grained structural components (curvature, torsion, compactness, contacts, etc.).
