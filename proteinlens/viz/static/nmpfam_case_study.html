SAE Features Generalize to Metagenomic Dark Matter

The NMPFams database contains over 100,000 protein families from microbial metagenomes and metatranscriptomes that have no or weak hits to Pfam or reference genomes. These proteins represent the functional "dark matter" of the biosphere — sequences unseen by standard annotation pipelines.

We sampled NMPFams families with AlphaFold2-predicted structures, ran their consensus sequences through ESM2 → SAE, and asked: do sparse, geometry-primary SAE features — the ones that survived confound filtering — fire on these novel proteins? Features in this triple intersection detect genuine structural motifs that transcend the SwissProt training distribution.
